# A2 claim ledger — independent reviewer classification

Levels: SUPPORTED (evidence executed and enclosing), CONDITIONAL (true under
stated conditions, not yet demonstrated where claimed), EXPLORATORY
(indicative, out-of-comparison), UNSUPPORTED (no adequate evidence).

## SUPPORTED

- S1 Matched observation cannot increase Fisher information (Theorem 1,
  full-joint). Synthetic matched/mismatch models plus physical N=8 Loewner
  check (10 seeds, quadrature): supported.
- S2 Dual spectra share 1-c_i^2; the 3/4, 1, 0 nonmonotone-retention example.
  Re-derived and recomputed in this review: supported.
- S3 Nested nuisance loss, neutral-admission kernel, complex safe kernel
  (Theorems 5/6). Exact fixtures + 36 seeded tangent checks: supported as
  finite-rank algebra.
- S4 Bias-aware worst-case risk identity (Theorem 7). Synthetic Monte Carlo:
  supported as fixed-linear-model algebra; physical evaluability 0/20.
- S5 Shared-map innovation, budget identity, two-frame counterexample
  (Theorem 9/Corollary 10). Exact controls and 24 budget records: supported.
- S6 Passive-medium resolvent bound (Proposition 1). Derivation re-verified
  this pass; 12 positive-loss cases enclose independent dense evaluations;
  6 lossless and 1 extreme-contrast case refuse: supported as stated
  (model-conditional, floating-point diagnostic).
- S7 E4 data integrity: 2,400 uniquely paired complete records, budget
  caps honored, r_free=0, L_det unused, estimates at initial candidates.
  Verified against raw JSONL: supported.

## CONDITIONAL

- C1 Guarded SOM-informed reduction performance: UNTESTED — zero accepted
  reduced moves across all 2,400 E4 records.
- C2 Certificate affordability at high frequency: conditional-negative —
  rank-128 enriched bases unadmitted at k=4pi; low-frequency rank-72
  non-vacuity only.
- C3 Geometry calibration improves reconstruction: conditional on the
  larger-map family (49 Gaussians, full aperture, 30 dB, exact-direct, 6
  scenes). Not demonstrated for the guarded method.
- C4 Efficient Fisher contraction on physical tangents: conditional — theorem
  synthetic-verified; physical check is full-joint only.
- C5 Conditional descent (Theorem 12): hypotheses (step-metric bounds,
  relative gradient error) never verified inside E4.
- C6 Passive bound novelty: conditional/unestablished.
- C7 Measured-data cylinder geometry consistency (recovered ~26.5-27 mm
  radius vs published "about 30 mm"): conditional on a misspecified plane-wave
  model; eps_r non-identifiable.

## EXPLORATORY

- E1 Residual enrichment admission (16 reference-point probes, outside frozen
  comparison): state and gradient bounds enclose independent errors; no
  nonlinear consequence shown.
- E2 Mismatch controls (clock ramp, receiver coupling, outside-basis): single
  scenes exposing false-assurance potential; no prevalence estimate.
- E3 E5 tangent-level acquisition policies (greedy/pair/exhaustive agreement
  n=1; synthetic greedy gaps): tangent scores only, no nonlinear acquisition
  validation.

## UNSUPPORTED

- U1 Any superiority of SOM-informed reduction over matched direct inversion.
- U2 A universal calibration basin or a certified phase branch.
- U3 A SOM-exclusive information gain over direct full-wave coordinates.
- U4 Blind measured-array geometry recovery (no position-error ground truth;
  measured pilot does not claim it).
- U5 Any claim that intensity exceeds or matches coherent information.
- U6 Readiness for TAP or TGRS as an experimental/method paper (see
  DECISION.md).
