# ScholarQA prior-art search plan

Search date: 2026-09-03

Depth: deep review / broad discovery, not a systematic review. Semantic Scholar
is the primary academic source. The final novelty judgment remains with Codex
and is bounded to retrieved evidence.

> effective map information for full-wave volumetric inverse-scattering SLAM,

> multi-frequency shared-compensation effects, and robust trajectory

Screening priorities:

- retain the closest full-wave, coherent-wave, RF/radar SLAM, unknown-sensor,
  SOM/CSI, EFIM, bilinear-identifiability, and robust-design work;
- actively retain contradictory or overlapping work;
- do not infer substantive results from titles, citations, or graph edges alone;
- separate generic algebraic antecedents from papers matching the full physical
  combination;
- verify every paper used in the final manuscript or claim ledger.
