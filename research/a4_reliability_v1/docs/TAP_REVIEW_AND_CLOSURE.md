## Strict mock review

**Summary.** A carefully bounded radial-scatterer experiment yields an exact geometry sensitivity after material/gain profiling. Code and counterexamples are provided, including an explicit negative controller comparison.

**Novelty.** Potentially interesting closed-form specialization, but novelty versus classical electromagnetic dipole localization has not been demonstrated. The coverage and covariance methods are standard. The high-level 'phase attribution' framing is not enough.

**Technical correctness.** The stated analytic spectrum and symmetry argument are coherent under the modal/scalar-gain assumptions. The distinction between a proof and floating-point certification is correctly retained. Unknown support, general material tangents, illumination leakage and antenna coupling lie outside the result.

**Experimental quality.** Reproducible small examples, matched noise, strong simple controls and preserved failures are positives. Eight calibration scenes and a designed24-scene branch suite are not a broad method evaluation. Scalar Mie/Rayleigh coefficient comparison is not independent arbitrary-shape full-wave tomography. DDA stress is diagnostic; hardware feasibility and accurate error enclosures are absent.

**Major concerns.** No supported superior controller; incomplete full proof-level nearest-prior closure; ideal modal illumination and known support; no general-material coverage; explicit false acceptance under invalid discrepancy assumptions. The corrective reference audit is transparent but illustrates the need for independent implementation review.

**Recommendation.** Reject as the originally proposed general TAP method paper in its current state. Do not describe this as an externally received rejection. A narrower electromagnetic measurement-design/limits submission is only a possibility after priority and physical realizability are established; its readiness is not certified by changing the title.
