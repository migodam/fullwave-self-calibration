## Experiment protocol

First correctness, then development pilots, then freeze the final test protocol before inspecting final data. Separate development/tuning/test scenes. Paired scene-level inference; initializations within a scene are not independent scenes. Include failures. Preregister any sample expansion rather than optional stopping for significance.

Primary operational benefit thresholds: at least 20% end-to-end runtime reduction at matched accuracy, or 10 percentage points success improvement at matched budget. Declare noninferiority margins and confidence procedure before final tests. Distinguish confidence intervals excluding a benefit from intervals too wide to decide. No rank-only victory.

Compare optimized direct full-wave joint inversion (adjoint/matrix-free as appropriate), task-oriented ROM, Krylov/rational methods, SOM/Twofold with identical enrichment, and fixed-wrong-pose/known-pose/intensity controls. Compare MFSOM electronics calibration only on overlapping tasks, not by inventing absent geometry outputs. Hold final data fixed when ablating frequency continuation. Include material capacity, full/limited aperture, weak/strong scattering, translation/rotation initialization, noise and electronics/model mismatch.

Count basis construction/update, forward and adjoint solves, validation, fallback, acquisition choice, factorization, QR/SVD, matrix dimensions, wall time and peak memory. Separate cold cost from declared reusable costs. Formal timings run serially, not alongside CPU workers. Apple Silicon CPU; at most two compute jobs and approximately 16 GiB aggregate target memory. No CUDA/cloud purchases/new daemons.

## Failure-to-next-route rules

SOM unsupported -> remove exclusivity, retain validated full-wave attribution/branch/rejection contribution. Expensive strict certificate -> offline audit, explicitly approximate online guard. Acquisition fails -> withdraw superiority and retain verified identifiability/intervention conditions. Exact ambiguity -> estimate quotient/combinations or request minimal new measurement, never return fake precision. Prior-art collision -> controlled purpose/mechanism/evaluation revision and renewed comparison. An environmental interruption creates a truthful checkpoint, not completion.
