## Frozen distinctions

- Old 3D `heldout_phase_rmse_rad` is reference-solver parameter transfer, not
  deployable prediction. Actual inverse-model sensor predictions and recovered
  structural fields are separately stored in `results/prediction_audit3d.json`.
- Conditional noise replications of one bank are not independent scenes.
- Coarse-only inversion is an accuracy/cost endpoint, not an equal-fidelity win.
- Additional noisy electronics references are additional measurements, not free
  algorithmic information or true gains handed to the estimator.
- Bibliographic resolution, abstract inspection and full-text verification are
  different evidence tiers. ScholarQA retrieval failures are not novelty proof.
- Actual installed autonomous-pipeline execution is in PIPELINE_AUDIT.md; its
  drift, failed writeup and provider failures are not hidden by local follow-up.
