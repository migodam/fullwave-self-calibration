## Scientific motivation that survives these corrections (D, derivation/inference)

For an isolated propagation path of length L, a small path error gives phase error approximately k delta-L. A monostatic radial displacement changes the round-trip path approximately twice as much; bistatic derivatives depend on both directions. In multiple scattering, one must differentiate the actual field rather than substitute a single-path phase formula everywhere. Near a field zero, relative phase is poorly conditioned; use raw complex likelihood and amplitude-weighted phase evaluation.

Self-calibration is relevant when coherent samples exist, the dominant mismatch can be represented by identifiable low-dimensional parameters, and cross-view/frequency constraints keep those parameters from being absorbed by material/electronics. It is not a universal replacement for phase retrieval, hardware calibration or stable clocks. Independent arbitrary phase errors on every observation can destroy the information this method requires.

Retaining complex data before a deterministic magnitude transformation cannot reduce statistical information under the same observation model and priors. That data-processing observation does not prove a practical optimizer wins: an inaccurate coherent model can be worse than an appropriately modeled intensity likelihood. Nor does high frequency automatically improve identifiability: it may increase wrapping, model sensitivity and material complexity simultaneously.

The realistic narrow application target is quantitative near-field microwave characterization with movable/repositionable antennas, a sufficiently stationary object/background, and a repeatable electronic response or auxiliary reference. The measured outputs of interest are material contrast and reliable coherent prediction at unseen views—not simply a prettier focused image. Human tissue, through-wall scenes and vehicles add dispersion, motion, heterogeneous backgrounds, pattern/coupling errors and possibly multipath mechanisms not yet tested here. They remain extensions, not validation claims.
