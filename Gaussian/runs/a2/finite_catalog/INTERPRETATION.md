`0.7 < 1/sqrt(2)`.  A supported-new decision in this run therefore excludes
only the frozen finite old-shape catalog; it does not establish two resolved

certificate remaining unknown: old and split-0.3 are naive both-compatible,
and split-0.7 is naive supported-new.  There are no naive false positives in
